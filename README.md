**Leonida Ledger** — the complete guide and tracker for all 55 PlayStation 5 trophies of Grand Theft Auto VI (1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze, 28 hidden). Every trophy is shown with its official name, description and grade, plus a guide written in our own words with a named, linked source — GTABase for the roadmap, counts and unlock steps, Push Square and Output Lag for the missable warning and tracking details. The tracker saves your earned trophies in the browser (localStorage), with search, filters by kind (story, missable, collectibles, activities, online — there are no online trophies), grade and status, a spoiler setting that hides hidden/story trophies until you earn them, and export/import of your progress as a file. The roadmap walks the platinum in nine steps, flags the one possibly missable trophy (Positive Mental Attitude — the Chapter 1 balcony meditation), and lays out the full 100% completion checklist. Fully offline, no network requests, no official Rockstar artwork — the look is our own.
