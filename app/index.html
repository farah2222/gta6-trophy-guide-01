Roadmap to the platinum

One playthrough is enough. There is no difficulty setting, no mission replay and no New Game+, but free roam carries on after the story — so almost everything can be cleaned up after the credits. The plan below follows GTABase's roadmap, with the missable warning up front.

⚠ The one possibly missable trophy — read this first.

Positive Mental Attitude (meditate at all 18 spots) may be missable: in Chapter 1, the morning after Down to Earth, follow Lucia from the kitchen to the balcony and accept her meditation. It is gone after Chapter 1, and it is not confirmed yet whether the trophy needs it — so do not skip it. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission, because there is no mission replay. Sources: GTABase · Output Lag

  1. Play the story, chapters 1–2. Do Lucia's balcony meditation in Chapter 1 (see warning above). From Chapter 2, Jason and Lucia are both playable — most of the list opens up here. Turn on every progress and collectible notification in Settings.
  2. Scan as you go. WAiNK-scan vehicles (200 needed for 100%), study animals with binoculars (100), meditate with both characters, and grab WAiNK spots whenever you pass one. It is all easier now than in clean-up.
  3. Take side content as it appears. Character quest lines (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone, Bret) and stranger strands are offered for a while — start them when they show up. Stranger missions are not part of 100%, but Strangers & Freaks needs 8 strands.
  4. Finish the story, chapters 3–5. Story trophies pop as you play; chapter trophies can pop a mission or two into the next chapter. Do not push your Criminal Profile towards Irredeemable — it is permanent.
  5. After the credits, open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. Work through what is left: every robbery, all 8 bank vaults, all 39 gang operations, underwater discoveries, every Shooting Range gold, all drone races and Bret's 15 Target Decks. This is the longest stretch — around 100 hours.
  6. Finish the partial counts. 100 optional missions, 200 of 277 vehicle scans, 100 of 255 animal studies, 15 of 18 meditations, 70 of 80 challenges, 300 stunts and 100 WAiNK scans.
  7. Clean up the miscellaneous trophies. Partner robberies (Hot Together, Retail Therapy), Combat Focus (Gunslinger), the VCIA jet (Catch Me If You Can), the tank (Challenging Tank), jewelry heists (Sneakthief) and the money trophies (Dime on the Dollar, Treat Yo Self).
  8. Buy the garages, register the vehicles. Little Boxes (8 garages, ~$160,000) and On the Spot (10 Scooter Bros registrations) need garage space and cash — do them together.
  9. Platinum. Once A Long Road (100% completion) pops, Vice Got a Price! follows immediately.
